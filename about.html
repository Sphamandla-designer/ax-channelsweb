® Our story

We started because too much local design was playing it safe.

AX-Channels opened in a two-desk studio in Braamfontein in 2016 with one belief: ambitious South African brands deserve the same calibre of design as the global names they compete with. We began with brand identities, moved into web, and added mobile UX as our clients' products grew.

Nine years on we are still deliberately small. Every project is led by a senior designer who stays on it from the first workshop to launch. There is no sales team and no bench of juniors. What we have instead is time to think, craft and get the details right.

Today we work from Johannesburg and Cape Town with clients across finance, health, hospitality, energy and mobility. The work changes. The standard doesn't.

® What we believe

Four things
we won't compromise.

  1. 01

    Strategy before style

    We ask what the work needs to do before we ask what it should look like. Every visual decision traces back to a business outcome.

  2. 02

    Senior hands only

    The people in your kickoff are the people who design and build your project. No hand-offs, no diluted thinking.

  3. 03

    Craft in the details

    Type, spacing, motion, load time. The small things are the work. We ship nothing we wouldn't put our name on.

  4. 04

    Honest partnership

    We tell you when an idea won't work and show you progress every week. Clients come back because the process is clear.
