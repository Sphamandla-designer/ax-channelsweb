® About the studio

A studio witha point of view.

AX-Channels is a small, senior design studio from South Africa. Since 2016 we've built websites, mobile products and brand identities for companies that want to be taken seriously, and then taken further.

Johannesburg · Cape Town Web · Mobile · Brand Est. 2016

® Our story

We started because too much South African design was playing it safe.

AX-Channels opened in a two-desk studio in Braamfontein in 2016 with one belief: ambitious local brands deserve the same calibre of design as the global names they compete with. We began with brand identities, moved into web, and added mobile UX as our clients' products did.

Nine years on we're still deliberately small. Every project is led by a senior designer who stays on it from the first workshop to launch. We don't run a sales team or a bench of juniors. What we have instead is time to think, craft and get the details right.

Today we work from Johannesburg and Cape Town with clients across finance, health, hospitality, energy and mobility. The work changes. The standard doesn't.

® What we believe

Four values.Zero compromise.

  • 01

    Strategy before style

    We ask what the work needs to do before we ask what it should look like. Every visual decision traces back to a business outcome.

  • 02

    Senior hands only

    The people in your kickoff are the people who design and build your project. No hand-offs, no diluted thinking.

  • 03

    Craft in the details

    Type, spacing, motion, load time. The small things are the work. We ship nothing we wouldn't put our name on.

  • 04

    Honest partnership

    We'll tell you when an idea won't work, and we'll show you progress every week. Clients come back because the process is clear.
