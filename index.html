04 Our approach

Good design beginswith the problem.

Every project combines business requirements, user needs and practical implementation considerations.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We understand the business, its objectives, target audience, challenges and project requirements.

    You get a clear brief
  2. 02

    Strategy & Structure

    We establish the information architecture, user journeys, functionality and overall direction of the project.

    You get sitemaps & flows
  3. 03

    Design

    We develop the visual direction, interfaces, components and interactions needed for a consistent user experience.

    You get interfaces & prototypes
  4. 04

    Development & Implementation

    Where included in scope, we translate approved website designs into functional digital experiences.

    You get a working build
  5. 05

    Testing & Refinement

    We review functionality, responsiveness, usability and overall experience, and refine before delivery.

    You get a launch-ready product
